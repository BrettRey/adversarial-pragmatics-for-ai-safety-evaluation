## The division of labour

Three sources, three different jobs. Worth keeping straight:

| Source | What it is cited for |
|---|---|
| `Goodman1955` | the notion of projectibility itself |
| `reynolds2026projectibilityHistory` | the four-question separation (declaration / warrant / profile-and-ground / discovery-and-revision), the partial order over world-side claims, the prospective revision protocol |
| `reynolds2026kindsProjectibilityProfiles` | profiles, support grades, demotion rules |

Grounded in the two papers' own claims: `kinds` is subtitled *Support Grades and
Demotion Rules*, and the history paper credits it with "projectibility profiles
and the basic demotion strategy" while claiming the separation, the partial
order and the revision protocol for itself.
